ABOUT US

Super Smart Service Ltd (SSS) established in December 2015 in West Midlands, U.K. The operation started by providing reliable fulfilment warehousing solutions and prompt final mile distribution service for our cross-border e-sellers and UK local online retailers on eBay, Amazon and other platforms. Over the past few years, we have formed strong working relationship with partners like Royal Mail, Yodel, Hermes, China Post, DPD, XDP, Palletways as well as Goodcang, YunExpress and other logistic giants.

SSS operated 10+ warehouse sites across the West Midlands in Aldridge, Lichfield, Cannock, Burton-on-Trent, Wolverhampton and Daventry with a total capacity of over 2 million square feet. With the opening of Cannock site in September 2019, SSS brought £50 million boost to the regional economy and created more than 300+ new jobs. At our full operational capacity, SSS is able to process and dispatch 70,000 parcels daily as well as offloading 60+ 40ft sea containers every day.

Now by taking advantage of our advanced warehouse management system and State-of-art facilities, Super Smart Service’s specialist arm reaches to serve more UK domestic businesses and providing second-to-none storage and distribution service at reasonable prices to support the great restart of UK economy.
